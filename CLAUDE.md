## Writing style for agents

Write plainly. Do not use the stylistic tics that mark machine-generated text.
This applies to all repo prose: code comments, commit messages, PR titles and
descriptions, docs, and lesson content.

- No em dashes or en dashes (`—`, `–`). Use a comma, a colon, parentheses, or
  two sentences. Hyphens in compound words are fine.
- No "not just X, but Y" or "it's not only ..., it's ..." framing.
- Cut filler and hype words: delve, leverage, seamless, robust, crucial,
  essential, elevate, unlock, boost, streamline, "in today's ... world",
  "a testament to", "underscore", "furthermore", "moreover".
- No emoji in commits, PRs, or comments.
- No wind-up or sign-off boilerplate ("Certainly", "Great question", "I hope
  this helps"). State the point directly.
- Straight quotes, not curly.

Prefer short declarative sentences over triads and rhetorical flourish.
